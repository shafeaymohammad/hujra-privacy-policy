Privacy Policy for Hujra

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Hujra mobile application (package name: com.hujraapp.hujra) collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In (via Firebase Authentication), we receive your unique user identifier (UID), email address, and basic profile details (such as display name and profile photo) to authenticate your account.
  • App Activity Data: We store your watch history and saved videos in Google Cloud Firestore linked to your user ID so your progress and bookmarks stay synced.
  • Local Device Storage: We store app preferences (such as theme mode, onboarding status, notification read status, and cached watch history) locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you securely and maintain your session.
  • To sync and display your watch history and saved educational videos.
  • To provide app updates, notifications, and improve overall app performance.

We do not sell your personal data or serve third-party advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

Hujra uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In & Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore)
  • YouTube (Embedded Educational Video Content)

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between Hujra and Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your personal watch history and saved videos is restricted to your authenticated account.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You can sign out of your account at any time from the app's navigation drawer, which clears local cached history from your device. To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data (watch history and saved videos), please email us at [YOUR_EMAIL@example.com] with the subject line "Hujra Account Deletion Request" and include the email address used to sign in. We will process your request within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

Hujra is an educational app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
